# Las Cajas de Pandora

A comedic browser idle/clicker game. Pandora, a West Highland White Terrier, is a pompous industrialist building a vegan dog-food empire; the player clicks to produce and grows her company through stages.

## Language

**Pandora**:
The Westie boss and the game's main character; the thing the player clicks.
_Avoid_: the dog, the player, the cookie

**Las Cajas de Pandora**:
Pandora's vegan dog-food company, whose growth is the game's progression.
_Avoid_: the factory, the business

**Croquetas**:
The single currency: vegan kibble produced by clicking Pandora and by Producers.
_Avoid_: cookies, money, cajas

**Stage**:
One step of the company's growth, each with its own setting, look for Pandora, and Producers. The economy carries over between stages. The MVP has three: La Cocina, La Fábrica, La Multinacional.
_Avoid_: level, world, prestige

**Producer**:
A purchasable, repeatable item that generates Croquetas automatically; each Stage introduces four.
_Avoid_: building, generator

**Upgrade**:
A one-time purchase that permanently multiplies a Producer's or a click's output.

**Inversionista**:
The random bonus event: an investor who crosses the screen and grants a reward when clicked.
_Avoid_: golden cookie

**Expansion**:
The expensive purchase that moves the company to the next Stage.
_Avoid_: level-up, unlock

**News Ticker**:
Scrolling satirical headlines about the company, varying by Stage.

**Memo**:
The corporate announcement shown when an Expansion is bought.
_Avoid_: cutscene

**Quote**:
A speech bubble line spoken by Pandora when clicked or at milestones.
